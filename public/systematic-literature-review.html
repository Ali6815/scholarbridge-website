Research services

Literature Reviews

Search strategies, screening methods, synthesis and transparent systematic review workflows.

Support shaped around your study.

  • PRISMA 2020 reporting and protocol development
  • Academic database searching and search strategy development
  • Inclusion and exclusion criteria
  • Literature screening and exclusion logs
  • Research extraction matrices
  • Bibliometric analysis where appropriate
  • Thematic synthesis and evidence-based literature reviews

What you can expect

After an initial discussion, we propose a written scope, quotation, timeline and deliverables for your approval. Depending on the agreed scope, these may include an annotated review, documented analysis workflow, interpretation session or a methods action plan. Tool and method choices depend on the research question, data quality and available expertise.

Illustrative workflow · no study counts

A traceable review process.

  1. Identify records through documented database searches
  2. Remove duplicate records
  3. Screen titles and abstracts against eligibility criteria
  4. Assess full texts and document reasons for exclusion
  5. Include eligible studies and synthesize evidence

Simplified PRISMA 2020 workflow; a completed review requires the appropriate full checklist and flow diagram.
