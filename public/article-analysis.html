Understanding PLS-SEM Results

Partial least squares structural equation modeling can be useful for certain predictive and explanatory modeling contexts, but interpreting results requires more than reading p-values.

Review indicator quality, reliability, validity and collinearity before discussing structural relationships. Examine effect sizes, uncertainty intervals and predictive performance. Report unexpected findings rather than hiding them.

Cross-sectional associations should not automatically be called causal effects. Distinguish what the model estimates from what the research design can establish.

This information is general guidance and should be tailored to the specific research design.
