Privacy notice.

How ScholarBridge handles your contact and consultation enquiries.

Draft for business-owner and legal review · 17 November 2026

What this website collects

The enquiry form collects your name, email, country, research field, service selection, deadline, project description and consent, together with any optional phone number, organization, research level and consultation preferences you provide. Direct submissions are processed and stored through Netlify Forms, including spam filtering. Email or WhatsApp links open the corresponding application; messages sent there are handled by those providers.

How submitted details are used

If you send Scholar Bridge an enquiry, the contact details and research description you provide may be used to respond, discuss a potential engagement and retain relevant business correspondence. Do not include sensitive research datasets or confidential documents in the initial enquiry.

Cookies, analytics and retention

No analytics scripts or advertising cookies are added by this website. Hosting providers may process request logs and security information. A fixed retention schedule has not yet been adopted; the owner should set one and regularly review and delete enquiries no longer needed. This draft does not assert compliance with any particular privacy law.

Third-party services

This website uses Google Fonts and provides links to WhatsApp and email services. These providers may process technical data under their own policies. Netlify hosts this site and processes direct form submissions and associated technical information. Submitted enquiries are accessible to authorized site administrators. To request access, correction or deletion of your enquiry, contact the email below. Business correspondence is retained as needed to handle the enquiry and meet applicable obligations.
