Frequently asked questions

Answers before you begin.

Learn how our research consultancy services work and what you can expect from an initial enquiry.

Do you guarantee journal publication?

No. Journal decisions are made independently by editors and reviewers. We support ethical manuscript improvement and submission preparation, without promises of acceptance.

Can you write my thesis or assessed assignment?

No. Our services focus on consultation, methods guidance, editing, analytical interpretation and building independent research skills. We do not ghostwrite assessed academic work.

Which statistical tools do you support?

Services can include methodological guidance for SPSS, SmartPLS, R and Python, subject to project scope and available expertise.

Can I consult from outside the UAE?

Yes. Initial enquiries are accepted from international researchers, subject to scheduling and agreement on the scope.

How do I receive a price quotation?

Send a brief description of your project and required support. We review the request before proposing a scope and quotation.

Is my consultation automatically booked when I submit the form?

No. Form submission is an enquiry only. A consultation is confirmed after ScholarBridge responds and agrees to a meeting time.
