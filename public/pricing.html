How it works

From first enquiry to a clear plan.

We keep the process practical. You know what has been agreed, what happens next and how any payment is verified.

Start an enquiry ↗
01

Tell us about your research

Use the existing enquiry form to describe your topic, services needed and target timeline. Please do not submit sensitive datasets or unpublished documents through the public form.

02

Receive a personalized quotation

We review the request and, if suitable, agree the service scope, deliverables, turnaround, currency and fee in writing.

03

Arrange a consultation if needed

Choose from the 30-minute online booking calendar or request a consultation. Any fee and engagement details are confirmed separately.

04

Follow agreed payment instructions

Once you receive an invoice or written payment request, use the approved method. For eligible Pakistan transfers, see the SadaPay instructions. Never pay an unconfirmed amount.

05

Receive confirmation and begin

We verify receipt of funds where payment is required, then confirm the service arrangements and agreed next steps.

Service terms at a glance

Clear expectations, before work begins.

These points summarize our current website guidance. Your written quotation and service agreement should specify the project-specific terms.

Scope, fee & deliverables

An enquiry or calendar booking is not an automatic engagement. Services, fees, deadlines and deliverables are agreed separately in writing.

Changes, cancellation & refunds

Ask about cancellation, rescheduling and refund conditions before paying. No universal refund amount or deadline is promised here; applicable terms should be stated in your written agreement and follow applicable law.

Confidentiality & document handling

Share only a non-sensitive summary through public forms. We can agree an appropriate channel and handling arrangements before receiving research materials.

Academic integrity

We provide research guidance and editorial support, not ghostwriting, fabricated data, false authorship or guaranteed publication or academic results.
