Our principles

Research integrity is non-negotiable.

We believe academic support should improve independent research practice, not substitute for it.

What we support

Research planning, methods consultation, survey design guidance, statistical interpretation, literature-review methods, language and structural editing, and feedback that helps researchers make their own scholarly decisions.

What we do not offer

We do not offer ghostwriting for assessed work, fabrication or manipulation of data, false authorship, paid acceptance arrangements, fake peer reviews or guaranteed journal publication.

What our research stages mean

Submitted manuscript / editorial review: submitted for editorial consideration; this does not imply acceptance, completed peer review or publication.

Advanced manuscript draft: a substantial working manuscript that is still being refined. It is not presented as a submitted or published paper.

Developing research: data, model verification or research design is still in progress. Validated empirical findings are not claimed.

Stage labels follow the supplied portfolio information. Posters are educational summaries, not the underlying manuscripts or datasets. No numerical findings or third-party endorsements are asserted.

Client responsibility

Clients remain responsible for the integrity, originality, accuracy, authorship and ethical approvals applicable to their research, and for following their institution's rules and journal policies.
